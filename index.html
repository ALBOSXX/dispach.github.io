Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

At Dispach Team, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Dispach application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide the functionality of the Dispach messenger, we collect only the information necessary to operate the service.

  • Account Information: your username, display name, and email address required to create and use your account.
  • Messages and Attachments: messages and files are processed solely for the purpose of delivering them to their intended recipients. Data is transmitted over a secure connection.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device information, operating system version, and technical logs may be collected to diagnose issues, maintain security, and ensure the stability of the service.
  • Application Permissions: microphone, phone, and Foreground Service permissions are used exclusively during active voice calls to provide VoIP calling functionality through Android Telecom and ConnectionService.

2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • creating and maintaining your user account;
  • delivering messages, files, and voice calls;
  • ensuring user security and preventing abuse;
  • diagnosing technical issues and improving the stability and performance of the application.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your information may only be disclosed where required by applicable law or when necessary to provide the functionality of the service.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Dispach does not use advertising identifiers and does not collect data for personalized advertising.

All communication between the application and our servers is transmitted over secure connections.

Account information is retained until the user requests deletion of the account or as otherwise required by applicable law.

5. Your Rights

You may request the deletion of your account and the associated data at any time by contacting our support team.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with the updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: dispachmessaging@gmail.com.
